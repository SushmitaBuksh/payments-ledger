# About this site

I'm Sushmita Buksh, a payments business analyst at a global bank, working toward becoming a payments domain expert. This site is my learning in public: a free, structured course I assembled from the best openly available resources, and the articles I write as I work through it.

## Why a public site

Writing something down for a stranger is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it. Every module ends with an exercise; every article is my answer to one of those exercises, or an explainer I wished existed when I started.

## What you'll find

- **The course** — twelve modules covering payment systems basics, Indian rails (RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, UPI), Swift and gpi, correspondent banking, MT and ISO 20022 messages, rejects and returns, exceptions and investigations, payment hubs and host-to-host, and business-analysis skills. Each module ends with a short quiz.
- **Articles** — write-ups, comparison tables and message walkthroughs, starting with the 12-part series *How money moves*.

Everything linked from the course is free. If a link breaks or you know a better resource, please tell me on the [feedback page](../feedback/).
