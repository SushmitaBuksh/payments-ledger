# About this site

Payments Domain Guide is written by Sushmita Buksh, a payments business analyst at a global bank working toward becoming a payments domain expert. It is her learning in public: a free, structured course I assembled from the best openly available resources, and the articles I write as I work through it.

## Why a public site

Writing something down for a stranger is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it. Every module here ends with an exercise; every article is my answer to one of those exercises, or an explainer I wished existed when I started.

## What you'll find

- **The course** — twelve modules covering payment systems basics, Indian rails (RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, UPI), Swift and gpi, correspondent banking, MT and ISO 20022 messages, rejects and returns, exceptions and investigations, payment hubs and host-to-host, and business-analysis skills.
- **Articles** — write-ups, comparison tables and message walkthroughs.

Everything linked from the course is free. If a link breaks or you know a better resource, I'd like to hear about it.
